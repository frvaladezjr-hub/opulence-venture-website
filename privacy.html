Privacy Disclosure

How we handle information you share with us

This disclosure explains what we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected when a prospect submits information directly, or when a licensed agent submits information on a prospect's behalf.

Effective date: September 17, 2026

1. Who this disclosure applies to

This Privacy Disclosure applies to every visitor of opulenceventuregroup.com (the "Site"), including:

  • Prospects who complete a consultation request, calculator, contact form, careers inquiry, or any other form on the Site.
  • Licensed agents who submit information through the Agent Discovery Form or any other intake tool on behalf of a prospect they represent.
  • Prospects introduced by an agent, whose personal and financial information is entered into the Site by that agent as part of a case-intake or case-design request.

Opulence Venture Group ("we," "us," or "our") is the party responsible for information submitted through the Site.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information a prospect provides directly

When a prospect uses the Site's consultation request, contact, calculators, resources, careers, or scheduling tools, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact method.
  • General planning context, such as goals, timeline, business or professional role, and topics of interest.
  • Non-identifying financial inputs entered into educational calculators (for example, age, income targets, savings balances, and rate assumptions). Calculator inputs are used to display an on-screen estimate and are not stored with your identity unless you separately submit them in a form.
  • Any message, note, or attachment you choose to include.

2.2 Information an agent submits on behalf of a prospect

When a licensed agent uses the Agent Discovery Form or a similar intake tool to introduce a case, the agent may submit information about the prospect that can include:

  • The prospect's name, age or date of birth, state of residence, marital status, and household composition.
  • Occupation, business ownership details, and general professional background.
  • Income, net worth ranges, asset categories, existing coverage, business structure, and other planning-relevant financial context.
  • The nature of the case, the planning objective, readiness, timeline, and any coordination needed with the prospect's other professionals.
  • The submitting agent's own contact information, licensing context, and preferred hand-off path.

Agent responsibility. Any agent who submits information on behalf of a prospect represents to us that (a) they have a legitimate business reason to share the information, (b) they have obtained the prospect's permission to submit that information to Opulence Venture Group for case review and planning coordination, and (c) they will only submit information that is reasonably necessary for that purpose. Agents should not submit Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, account numbers, medical records, or copies of identification documents through Site forms.

2.3 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our infrastructure providers automatically log limited technical information when the Site is used, including IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages visited. This information is used to operate the Site, prevent abuse, and understand general usage patterns. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles.

3. How we use the information

Information submitted through the Site is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • Review a submitted case, coordinate with the submitting agent, and prepare planning discussion materials.
  • Deliver requested resources, calculator explanations, and educational follow-up.
  • Consider career and consultant inquiries.
  • Maintain records of the interaction, meet applicable regulatory and record-keeping obligations, and improve the Site.

We do not sell information submitted through the Site, and we do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

4. Who receives the information

Information you submit may be received by:

  • Opulence Venture Group personnel and its Advance Planning Division, including reviewers assigned to your inquiry or case.
  • The referring or submitting licensed agent, where the submission relates to a case that agent introduced.
  • Specialists coordinated for the case, such as licensed insurance, tax, or legal professionals, but only to the extent needed to review and support the planning discussion.
  • Service providers that operate the Site, such as our hosting, deployment, form-delivery, email, and scheduling providers, who process information on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulatory, legal, or law-enforcement authorities, when required by law, subpoena, or a legitimate investigation of misuse of the Site.

5. How submissions are delivered

Site forms are transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Case intake submitted through the Agent Discovery Form is routed by a server-side function to our internal review inbox, with the submitting agent's contact information attached so we can respond directly to that agent. Scheduling links open our third-party calendar tool, which collects the information you choose to enter there under its own terms.

6. How long information is kept

We keep information submitted through the Site for as long as it is reasonably needed to respond to the inquiry, work the case, meet record-keeping requirements applicable to insurance and financial-services activity, and defend our legitimate interests. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or archived in a secured manner.

7. How the information is protected

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information submitted through the Site, including encryption in transit, access limits, and vendor selection focused on providers with recognized security practices. No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is entirely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

  • Access, correction, or deletion. A prospect may ask us to confirm what we hold, correct inaccurate information, or delete information submitted through the Site, subject to our regulatory record-keeping obligations. Agents may make the same request for their own submitting information.
  • Withdrawing consent. A prospect whose information was submitted by an agent may withdraw permission for us to continue reviewing that case at any time. Once we receive that request, we will stop active case work and, where appropriate, remove the information from active review.
  • Communication preferences. You may ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying to any message from us or using the contact details below.

9. California and other state privacy rights

Residents of California and certain other states may have rights to know what personal information is collected about them, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. Financial privacy notice

Because Opulence Venture Group facilitates insurance and financial-services planning, non-public personal information collected in connection with a case is treated consistent with applicable financial-privacy rules. We collect this information only to evaluate and support the requested planning, share it only with the parties described in Section 4, and require our service providers to safeguard it.

11. Children

The Site is intended for adults evaluating business, tax, retirement, insurance, and legacy planning. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through the Site, please contact us so we can remove it.

13. Updates to this disclosure

We may update this Privacy Disclosure to reflect changes in our practices, our providers, or applicable law. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on the Site for a reasonable period after they take effect.

This Privacy Disclosure is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. It describes current practices in effect as of the date shown above and should be read together with any additional notices, engagement documents, or carrier and product disclosures that apply to a specific case.
