Business Consulting

Business Consulting

Overview

Running a business involves a long list of structural, tax, and continuity decisions — many of which are made once and rarely revisited. Our business consulting work is designed to help you evaluate those decisions with a longer-term view.

We work with business owners to review how their business is structured, how key employees and executives are retained and compensated, and how the business would continue to operate if something unexpected happened to a key person or owner.

Business OwnersEntrepreneursPartners & Co-OwnersExecutive Teams
Areas of Focus

What we help you plan for

A review of your entity structure and how it aligns with your current tax situation, liability exposure, and growth plans.

Identifying business-level strategies that may help improve tax efficiency, subject to applicable rules and your specific facts.

Designing benefit and incentive strategies intended to help attract and retain key talent.

Planning for how ownership and leadership will transition, whether to family, partners, employees, or an outside buyer.

Evaluating strategies designed to help protect the business against the loss of an owner or critical employee.

Reviewing buy-sell agreements and their funding mechanisms to help support a smooth ownership transition.

Designing supplemental benefit strategies intended to help retain and reward key executives.

“A business built without a succession or continuity plan is a plan left to chance.”
